Windows local text recognition

Kiri includes the unmodified nbocr 0.2.3 Windows x64 executable by ChenZibo,
with PP-OCRv6 small detection and recognition models from PaddleOCR
and the MNN inference engine from Alibaba. The bundled Apache License 2.0
is in LICENSE alongside this notice.

Newbee OCR CLI     https://github.com/zibo-chen/newbee-ocr-cli/tree/v0.2.3
Rust Paddle OCR    https://github.com/zibo-chen/rust-paddle-ocr/tree/v2.3.1
PaddleOCR          https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR
MNN                https://github.com/alibaba/MNN
