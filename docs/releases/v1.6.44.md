## English

- Fixed: on Windows, **Print Screen** (bare or with modifiers) can now be recorded directly as a global shortcut in **Settings → Shortcuts** without a typing-key warning. Thanks to @tiger8888 for reporting (#209).
- Improved reliability when resuming interrupted in-app update downloads and cancelling in-progress OCR requests.

## 中文

- 修复：Windows 上现在可以在「设置 → 快捷键」中直接将 **Print Screen**（单独或组合键）录制为全局快捷键，且不再弹出打字键确认提示。感谢 @tiger8888 反馈（#209）。
- 提升了应用内更新断点续传下载以及取消进行中 OCR 识别时的稳定性。
