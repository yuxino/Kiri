## English

- Cancel MP4-to-GIF conversion while the video is being checked or converted. The original MP4 stays in your library.
- Check video decoding before GIF encoding, so decoding errors appear sooner.
- Fix GIF conversion failing near the end when the audio lasts longer than the video.

Contributors: @yuxino.

## 中文

- 素材库中的 MP4 转 GIF 支持在检查和转换阶段取消，原始视频保留。
- 编码前先检查视频能否解码，更早提示解码问题。
- 修复音频比视频更长时，GIF 转换到后段失败的问题。

本次发布贡献者：@yuxino。
