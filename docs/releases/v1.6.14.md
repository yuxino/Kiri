## English

- Choose from seven interface languages in Settings: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, German, Korean and French.
- Cancel MP4-to-GIF conversion while the video is being checked or converted. The original MP4 stays in your library.
- Check video decoding before GIF encoding, so decoding errors appear sooner. Fix conversion failing near the end when audio lasts longer than the video.

Contributors: @yuxino.

## 中文

- 设置中可选择七种界面语言：英文、简体中文、繁体中文、日文、德文、韩文和法文。
- 素材库中的 MP4 转 GIF 支持在检查和转换阶段取消，原始视频保留。
- 编码前先检查视频能否解码，更早提示解码问题；修复音频比视频更长时，转换到后段失败的问题。

本次发布贡献者：@yuxino。
